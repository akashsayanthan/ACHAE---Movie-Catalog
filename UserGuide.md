# REEL — User Guide

REEL is a film catalog app powered by TMDB. Browse popular movies, search by title, filter by genre, and leave reviews.

---

## Browsing Films

When you open the app, the most popular films load automatically in a grid. No account is needed.

- Click any card to open the full detail view — synopsis, rating, vote count, and reviews
- Scroll down to see more films on the current page
- Use the **← PREV** and **NEXT →** buttons at the bottom to move between pages

---

## Searching

1. Type a movie title into the search bar in the top right
2. Click SEARCH or press Enter
3. Results update automatically
4. Click CLEAR to go back to the popular films feed


---

## Filtering by Genre

Click any genre button in the filter bar (Action, Drama, Horror, etc.) to see films from that category. The app fetches a full set of results from TMDB for that genre — not just the current page.

Click ALL to return to the popular films feed.

---

## Creating an Account

1. Click SIGN IN in the top right
2. Click Register here
3. Enter a username, email address, and password (minimum 6 characters)
4. Click CREATE ACCOUNT
5. You will see a confirmation message — you can then sign in with your credentials

---

## Signing In

1. Click SIGN IN in the top right
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click SIGN IN

Your username will appear in the header. Your session is saved, you will stay logged in if you refresh the page.

To sign out, click SIGN OUT in the top right.

---

## Writing a Review

You must be signed in to write a review.

1. Click any film card to open its detail view
2. Scroll down to the REVIEWS section
3. Click WRITE A REVIEW
4. Select a star rating (1–5)
5. Write your review (minimum 10 characters)
6. Click SUBMIT REVIEW

Your review will appear in the list immediately with your username and the date.

---

## Deleting a Review

You can only delete your own reviews.

1. Open the film detail modal
2. Find your review in the list
3. Click the DELETE button next to it

The review is removed immediately.

---

## Admin Features

Admin users have additional controls not visible to regular users.

| Feature | How to use |
|---|---|
| Add a film | Click ADD FILM in the header, fill in the form, click ADD FILM |
| Edit a film | Click EDIT on any film card, update the fields, click SAVE CHANGES |
| Delete a film | Click DELETE on any film card and confirm |

Admin access is set by the site owner.
